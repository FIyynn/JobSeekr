## Compensation Interpretation

Target compensation path:

- 35,000+ AED/month by Year 2
- 35k–45k AED/month within 2–4 years

Do not treat 35k–45k as guaranteed current market salary for every role.

Score based on compensation trajectory:

- Strong immediate salary
- Strong graduate/analyst progression
- Elite brand with strong exits
- High-upside sector
- Direct path to investment, quant, AI, or strategic roles

Manual review if salary is unclear but the role is high-signal.

## Certification / Education Caution

Do not claim CFA certification unless true.

Allowed wording if accurate:

- “Finance and investment self-study”
- “CFA-style finance preparation”
- “Exposure to financial modeling and investment analysis”
- “Private equity and active investments internship experience”

Do not write:

- “CFA certified”
- “CFA Level I passed”
- “CFA candidate”

unless explicitly true.

Do not claim a master’s degree.

Do not auto-fail roles requesting a master’s degree unless the posting says it is mandatory.

## Application Behavior

When using this enhanced layer:

- Prioritize role quality over quantity.
- Prioritize ADIA, ADIC, Mubadala, ADQ, EIA, Lunate, hedge funds, AI, space, defense, and energy/commodities roles.
- Apply aggressively to analyst, associate, graduate, junior, researcher, and trainee roles.
- Save for manual review when a role is promising but slightly senior, unclear, or requires DM/referral.
- Skip weak software/devops roles unless they are connected to strong companies or target industries.
- Do not exaggerate AI/ML/LLM/PyTorch experience.
- Do not claim professional live trading.
- Do not claim full-time employment where experience was internship-based.
